Questions

Before you register

Do you guarantee an internship?

No. We make your applications as strong as possible and help you reach interviews. Offers depend on each employer's decision and your performance.

Who is this service for?

International students in the UK on a Student visa, students in Ireland on Stamp 2, Stamp 1 and Stamp 1G holders, and recent graduates, including those on the UK Graduate Route, who want internship experience in the UK or Ireland.

Can I do an internship on a Student visa or Stamp 2?

Usually yes, within your visa conditions. UK Student visa and Irish Stamp 2 holders have limits on working hours during term time, with more flexibility in official vacation periods. Always check the conditions on your own permission; we plan applications around them.

I'm a recent graduate on Stamp 1G or the Graduate Route. Is an internship worth it?

Often, yes. A graduate internship gives you UK or Irish experience and references during your permission period, which helps when you move on to a full-time role. We focus on roles that can lead to longer-term employment.

How much does it cost and how do I pay?

The service fee is £150, paid once. After you register we send you a secure Razorpay payment link by email or WhatsApp, and it also appears on this page when you return. Our team starts once the payment is confirmed.

Do you apply on my behalf?

With your approval. You see the shortlist first, and we tell you which roles were submitted and when.

Which fields do you cover?

Data and analytics, business analysis, finance and accounting, marketing, HR, supply chain and logistics, engineering, IT and software testing, and more. Tell us your course and we'll say if it's a fit.

How will I get updates?

By email or WhatsApp, and on this page: return here signed in to see your current stage and the latest note from our team.
