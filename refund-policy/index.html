Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

  1. Before work starts. If you cancel before we begin working on your order, we refund 100% of your payment.

  8. How to request a refund. Email hr@resumecraftinghub.com or WhatsApp +91 63692 25896 with your name and payment reference.
  9. Refund timeline. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.
