Job summary

The Irish Coast Guard (IRCG) is undergoing a significant programme of organisational transformation, and an opportunity now exists for Deputy Director - Coastal Units Branch to support this transformation within one of the Coast Guard’s four divisional areas - the IRCG Service Division. 

Main Duties of the Job

The Deputy Director will report to the Director of the Irish Coast Guard, under the overall responsibility of the Assistant Secretary General for the Irish Coast Guard. This is a senior leadership role within a unique operational environment. The Deputy Director-Coastal Units Branch will have national responsibility for the leadership, management and oversight of the Coast Guard Unit command structure and the volunteer experience within the IRCG. 

The successful candidate will bring substantial senior management experience in a complex operational or service delivery environment, with a strong record of leading people, working collaboratively and delivering results in the public interest. 

Please see the Candidate Information Booklet for full details of the role including specific eligibility requirements.

 

Information about the organisation

The IRCG is a pillar of the Department of Transport. Its mission is to deliver a world class search and rescue, maritime casualty, and pollution response service.
