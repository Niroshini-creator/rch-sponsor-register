    Data sources & last run status

    Adverts from recruitment agencies and consultancies are removed. Jobs come from employers' own careers sites, official boards (NHS Jobs, Teaching Vacancies, jobs.ac.uk, Times Higher Education, EURAXESS, Platsbanken) and, when switched on under Apply via, general job boards. Hiring contacts (name, job title, email, phone) are only shown when the employer published them with the advert, or when they were added by hand to config/contacts.json with a source link. Addresses are never guessed. Always verify sponsorship with the employer before applying.

    Employer tiers

    Size bands (UK Companies Act): small < 50 staff, medium 50–249, large 250+.

    Visa routes

    PSW: UK Graduate visa
    After a UK degree you can work for any employer for 2 years (3 after a PhD) without sponsorship. Jobs marked PSW friendly either say they accept the Graduate visa, or are entry-level roles at a licensed sponsor, so you can switch to a Skilled Worker visa later.
    OPT: US Optional Practical Training
    F-1 graduates can work for 12 months (36 with the STEM extension, which needs an E-Verify employer). Jobs marked OPT friendly either mention OPT / STEM OPT / cap-exempt H-1B, or are entry-level roles at an employer on the USCIS H-1B data or that uses E-Verify.
    English-speaking
    The advert is written in English and does not ask for another language (a language "is a plus" is fine).
    Ireland
    Ireland publishes no sponsor register, so Irish jobs appear when the advert offers sponsorship or an employment permit (Critical Skills or General Employment Permit).
    Rarely sponsored roles
    Front-line roles such as passenger service, check-in, gate, baggage and ramp agents, reservations, customer service advisers, administrators, receptionists, retail or hospitality jobs and apprenticeships seldom meet sponsorship skill and salary levels, so they are hidden unless you untick the option.
    UAE (Dubai)
    UAE employers sponsor the residence and work visa of every foreign hire, so every UAE job is listed.
