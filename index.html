    Data sources & last run status

    Adverts from recruitment agencies and consultancies are removed. Jobs come from employers' own careers sites, official boards (NHS Jobs, Teaching Vacancies, jobs.ac.uk, EURAXESS) and, when switched on under Apply via, general job boards. Contact emails are only shown when the employer published them in the advert, or when they were added by hand to config/contacts.json with a source link. Addresses are never guessed. Always verify sponsorship with the employer before applying.

    Employer tiers

    Size bands (UK Companies Act): small < 50 staff, medium 50–249, large 250+.
