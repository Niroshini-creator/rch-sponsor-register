Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

ResumeCrafting Hub Pvt Ltd ("we", "us") provides CV writing, interview preparation and job search support. This policy explains how we handle your personal information.

  1. What we collect. Your name, contact details (email, phone, WhatsApp), CV and career information, target roles, job links you share, and payment references. We do not store card details; payments are processed by our payment partners (such as Razorpay).
  2. Why we use it. To deliver the services you order, contact you about your order, process payments, and meet legal and tax obligations.
  3. Who we share it with. Only with those needed to deliver your service: our payment processors, and employers or job portals when you ask us to apply on your behalf. We never sell your data.
  4. How long we keep it. We keep your documents for up to 12 months after your last order so we can handle revisions and repeat orders, then delete them. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your documents sooner.
  5. Keeping it safe. We limit access to your documents to the team members working on your order.
  6. Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by contacting hr@resumecraftinghub.com. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have rights under UK GDPR / GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
  7. Testimonials. We only publish a testimonial or placement mention with your permission, and we don't publish your full name or documents.
  8. Contact. hr@resumecraftinghub.com, ResumeCrafting Hub Pvt Ltd, India.
