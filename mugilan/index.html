    Data sources & last run status

    Match % is a transparent keyword score against the CV: title 40, OSS/BSS and telecom keywords 35, network technology 10, architecture / GCP 5, seniority 5, pay 5, minus points for junior roles, pay below expectation and unverified sponsorship or work permit. Adverts needing SC / DV clearance or refusing sponsorship are removed, and so are French-language adverts. Reed and Adzuna are job boards, so agency adverts are kept but labelled; career-site roles are the employer's own. LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor offer no public search API and forbid scraping, so they are not read directly. Always confirm sponsorship, work permit and pay with the employer.

    Visa and work permit labels

    Advert offers sponsorship
    The advert says visa sponsorship is available.
    Licensed UK sponsor
    The advert is silent, but the employer is on the UK Home Office register of licensed sponsors.
    France: employer work permit
    France has no public sponsor register. A French employer applies for the permit (for example Talent Passport for a qualified employee, or an EU Blue Card), which depends on salary and role. Confirm with the employer.
    Not verified / Agency
    The employer is not on the register or the advert is from an agency, so sponsorship is unknown. These lose 5 points.
