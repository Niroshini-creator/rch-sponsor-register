CALL SCRIPT – employer (aim: book a 10-minute follow-up or get permission to send profiles)

1. "Hi, is that ${first_name}? It's ${sender_name} from ${sender_company}. I emailed you last week about candidates
   with the right to work. Have I caught you at a bad time?"
   (If bad time: ask when to call back and log outcome 'callback'.)
2. "We place graduates and professionals who already hold UK Graduate visa, Dependant visa or Irish Stamp 1G,
   so in many cases there's no sponsorship needed. Which roles are you finding hardest to fill right now?"
3. Listen. Offer: "Can I send you two or three anonymised profiles for that?" → log 'interested'.
4. If they say no: thank them and ask "Would you like me to take you off our list?" If yes → log 'do_not_call'.

Must say on every call: your name, ${sender_company}, and if asked, our address: ${sender_address}.
Never press if they ask not to be called again. Note: ${notes}
